Orthopaedic surgery at Invermay Vet Clinic

Invermay Vet Clinic is proud to be able to offer specialised orthopaedic surgeries at affordable prices. This includes bone and ligament repairs in dogs and cats.

Our surgeon, Dr Darren Taylor, has been performing orthopaedic surgeries for over 20 years and has done well over 50 TPLO procedures.

We offer fixed-fee packages that include all standard follow-up visits, pain medication, and xrays (for some procedures). Complications are rare, but can incur extra charges if further interventions are required.

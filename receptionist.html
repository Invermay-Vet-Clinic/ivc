Job description

Receptionist (Casual)

We are looking for a warm, organised, and reliable receptionist to join our friendly team on a casual basis, with the opportunity to move into a permanent role in the future.

As the first point of contact for our clients and their pets, you’ll play an important role in creating a welcoming and efficient front desk environment.

About the role:

  • Answering phones and responding to enquiries
  • Booking and managing appointments
  • Updating client and patient records
  • Assisting clients with forms and paperwork
  • Helping with basic pet handling and supporting the clinical team when needed

About you:

  • Calm and efficient under pressure
  • Confident on the phone and with face-to-face customer service
  • Well organised with strong attention to detail
  • Comfortable around animals and happy to assist with pet handling
  • Previous reception or customer service experience preferred

If you enjoy working with people and animals and want to be part of a caring, community-focused clinic, we’d love to hear from you.

To apply: Please send your resume and a short cover letter to admin@invermayvetclinic.com.

Job description

Veterinary nurse – Orthopaedic experience (Full-time)

Invermay Vet Clinic is seeking an experienced and motivated Veterinary Nurse to join our small team. The role is full-time and hours are negotiable for the right candidate.

This position is ideal for a nurse with a strong interest and proven experience in orthopaedic surgery who enjoys working in a hands-on clinical environment and providing high-quality patient care.

About the role:

  • Assisting with orthopaedic and general surgical procedures
  • Preparing and monitoring patients before, during and after anaesthesia
  • Surgical nursing, instrument handling, and theatre support
  • Inpatient care and recovery monitoring
  • Client communication and discharge instructions
  • Training other nurses
  • General nursing duties and teamwork across the clinic

About you:

  • Qualified Veterinary Nurse with at least Cert IV in vet nursing and relevant clinical experience
  • Confident assisting with orthopaedic surgeries
  • Strong anaesthesia monitoring skills
  • Calm, organised, and able to work efficiently in a busy environment
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills
  • Passionate about patient care and continuous learning

To apply: Please send your resume and a short cover letter to admin@invermayvetclinic.com.
